# Teletalk Autofill — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Teletalk Autofill is a browser extension that fills job application forms with details you
save in it. It is not affiliated with Teletalk Bangladesh Ltd.

## What the extension stores

The details you type into its settings page — such as your name, parents' names, date of
birth, national ID, birth registration or passport number, phone, email, addresses and
education results — plus the photo and signature images you choose.

They are saved only in your browser's local extension storage on your device.

## Where your data goes

- **Nowhere, by the extension.** It has no server, sends no data over the network, and
  contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code.
- When you click the toolbar button, it copies your details into the form fields of the page
  open in that tab. It never submits the form: you review it and decide whether to submit
  it to that website yourself.
- **Export backup** creates a file on your device only when you click it. Where that file
  goes afterwards is up to you.

## Sharing and sale

Your data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and is not used for any purpose
other than filling in forms at your request.

## Deleting your data

Click **Remove** next to the photo or signature, or clear the details on the settings page.
Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
